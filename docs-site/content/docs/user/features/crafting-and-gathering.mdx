Planning works on its own. Crafting and gathering go through **Artisan** and **GatherBuddy Reborn**, so their tools are only offered while those plugins are loaded, with their own modules under **Integrations maintained by XIV MCP**.

| Tool | What it does | Module |
|---|---|---|
| `plan_craft` | Plans a project: recipe tree, crafting order, stock from bags and retainers, where to get missing materials, bag space and batches, and the stats Artisan will craft with | Game data |
| `get_crafting_lists` / `set_crafting_list` / `delete_crafting_list` | Artisan's lists and state; creates, edits or deletes lists (lists XIV MCP writes skip what your bags already hold, so a list started again after a stop doesn't craft everything twice; a list from `prepare_craft_plan` always crafts its full quantities, since `count_existing` already subtracts what you have, and `run_crafting_list` continues it after a stop) | Artisan |
| `craft_item` / `crafting_control` | Crafts an item a number of times and waits until Artisan is done, then reports what was made (see [Crafting one item](#crafting-one-item)); starts, pauses, resumes or stops a list | Artisan |
| `prepare_craft_plan` | Creates the Artisan list in the right order and works out each recipe's steps ahead of time with **Raphael** (the crafting solver built into Artisan), with the exact stats Artisan will use | Artisan |
| `run_crafting_list` | Runs an Artisan list to the end and reports what was crafted per recipe. Stops and fails when Artisan gets stuck, and fails when the list ends with crafts still missing (for example because the bags filled up), so a job waits instead of going on short. A retainer list that a retainer plugin opens meanwhile is closed | Artisan |
| `get_gather_lists` / `set_gather_list` / `delete_gather_list` / `set_auto_gather` | GatherBuddy Reborn's auto-gather lists, and auto-gather on or off | GatherBuddy Reborn |
| `gather_until` | Gathers until your bags hold the target quantities. Your other lists are paused and restored afterwards. It can also stop on something else in your bags, such as the aethersand that GatherBuddy reduces collectables into. If GatherBuddy turns itself off between timed node windows, it is started again. A window that holds your character, such as the retainer list a retainer plugin opens at the bell, is closed first. If GatherBuddy hangs, so your character stands idle for 6 minutes (`stall_minutes`) while no count changes, the step fails and names the items still missing and what GatherBuddy last said. Skip those items or get them another way. Waiting for a timed node does not count as hanging. A session length ends the step without failing, so a job can take breaks between `gather_until` steps. Fallback items, such as a fish, are only gathered while nothing else on the list can be, for example between timed node windows. | GatherBuddy Reborn |

## Crafting one item

`craft_item` hands the crafts to Artisan and follows them: it counts the item in your bags as each craft finishes, collectables included. It is done when Artisan is done and has closed the crafting log (Artisan does that by default, and `craft_item` waits at most about 10 seconds for it), so the next step of a job finds your character free. Like `run_crafting_list`, it first leaves an open gathering node (the game refuses crafting while one is open). During a fight, Artisan cannot change your job: a job step waits for the fight to end, and a direct call is refused, so finish the fight first.

- **As a job step,** it waits for as long as the crafting takes. If Artisan stops with crafts still missing, the step fails and says how many were made and the likely cause: missing materials, full bags, or the wrong job or gear. If Artisan stays busy but nothing gets crafted for `stall_minutes` (default 5), Artisan is stopped and the step fails. The same happens when the Raphael solver works on one craft for 3 minutes; the step then runs again by itself. Pausing or cancelling the job stops Artisan too. A craft that is already under way still finishes and is counted. When the step runs again, it only crafts what is still missing.
- **Called directly,** it answers when Artisan is done, at the latest about 40 seconds after your assistant called it, because AI apps stop waiting for a tool call after about a minute. The time you take to approve it counts too. If Artisan is not done by then, your assistant hears how many crafts are made so far, and Artisan goes on crafting by itself. For more than a few crafts, your assistant should use a job step.

## Filling the bags

With `"quantity": "fill"`, `plan_craft` plans as many as fit in your bags. For collectables, it also plans no more than can be turned in before the scrip cap, with the scrips per collectability tier.

## Is your gear good enough?

`prepare_craft_plan` plays each prepared solution through Artisan's simulator with the stats Artisan will craft with, assuming normal conditions, and shows what it reaches (for collectables, the tier and collectability). If a solution doesn't finish the craft, or a collectable stays below the lowest tier, it refuses before anything is crafted: better gear, food or a potion is needed first. `plan_craft` shows the same for solutions already prepared.

## Where materials come from

`plan_craft` lists gil vendors only when a gil shop sells the material. Vendors that take other currencies, such as gemstones or scrips, are listed as exchanges. Some of them only trade once you have unlocked them. Tradeable materials also list the market board. When a vendor won't trade, `buy_item` passes on what the game says: its system and error messages, not combat lines or chat that arrive at the same time.

## Equipment as an ingredient

Some recipes use a piece of equipment as an ingredient, for example Wrapped Crowsbeak Hammer (a Steel Crowsbeak Hammer) or Engraved Hard Leather Grimoire (a Hard Leather Grimoire). Artisan can't select equipment in the crafting log: the ingredient stays "Unselected" and the synthesis never starts. So:

- `plan_craft` marks these steps with `craftByHand`, and `prepare_craft_plan` warns about them and marks them in the list. Craft them yourself: open the recipe, select the ingredient and start the synthesis.
- `craft_item` refuses such recipes instead of hanging.
- `run_crafting_list` notices when Artisan stays busy but nothing gets crafted (no bag counts change) for `stall_minutes` (default 5). It stops Artisan and fails with the recipe it is stuck on and the likely cause. A paused list or a solution still being worked out doesn't count as stuck, unless the Raphael solver works on one craft for 3 minutes without finishing (it can hang, for example on a recipe started with HQ ingredients). Then Artisan is stopped as well, and in a job the step runs again by itself and continues with the crafts still missing.

## A whole project as one job

> "Craft as many of this collectable as I can turn in before the scrip cap, gather what is missing, and turn them in."

Your assistant can run this as one [background job](/docs/user/features/jobs):

1. `prepare_craft_plan` with `"quantity": "fill"` for a collectable,
2. `gather_until` or `transfer_retainer_items` for the materials,
3. `run_crafting_list`,
4. `turn_in_collectables` (stops before the scrip cap),
5. `buy_item` with a standing approval, and
6. `sell_item`.

## Pausing and resuming a crafting step

Artisan always starts a list from the top. So when a job's `run_crafting_list` step runs again, after a pause or a retry, it continues instead: it remembers how many crafts of each recipe it already made (counted from the products arriving in your bags) and shrinks the Artisan list to the crafts still missing before it starts. Once everything is made, the list gets its original quantities back. The report always covers the whole list, including crafts from earlier runs. A new `run_crafting_list` step that replaces a failed or skipped one in the same job (for example after `update_job` replaced the remaining steps to gather a missing material first) continues the same way. If you edit the list in between, or the earlier step finished it, the step starts over with the list as it is.

`craft_item` does the same for its one item: when its step runs again, Artisan is only asked for the crafts still missing, with the same recipe even if your job changed in between. The rule for new steps is the same as for `run_crafting_list`: a new `craft_item` step for the same item and amount after a failed or skipped one in the same job continues that one, even if it was meant as a second batch.

## Monster drops

`farm_monster_drops` gets materials that only monsters drop: it switches to your combat gearset with the highest item level (or the one you name; if a set can't be equipped, for example because its weapon is gone, it tries the next best), travels to where the monster lives (from Teamcraft's drop data), targets the nearest living one, walks into range and lets RotationSolver fight in its auto mode, until your bags hold the quantity you asked for. A monster it cannot walk up to (behind trees or a cliff) is flown to where the zone allows flying; if that fails too, it is left alone for a few minutes and another one is taken. Between kills it waits at the spawn area for respawns, and with less than 70% HP it rests before pulling the next one. Whatever else attacks you meanwhile is fought back: it targets the nearest monster that has you targeted and keeps RotationSolver on. It stops when you die, at the timeout, or after a while without a kill or a drop; a fight still going on is finished first, then RotationSolver is switched off again. It needs RotationSolver and vnavmesh.
## Good to know

Stopping a crafting list (`crafting_control` with `stop`, or pausing or cancelling a job while it crafts) ends the list like Artisan's **Cancel** button. Artisan's own stop request would only pause it and keep Artisan busy.

Neither plugin offers a way to change its lists from outside. So editing a list briefly unloads the plugin, updates its file (with a backup) and loads it again. The Raphael preparation uses Artisan's internals and may need an update after Artisan changes.
